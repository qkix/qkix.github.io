Brick Tanks – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Brick Tanks (“the app”) is a game published by qkix (“we”). This policy explains what information is processed when you play it.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or any account of our own. The only data that reaches our own server are the optional, anonymous gameplay statistics described below – and only if you agree to send them.

The app saves your best score, furthest stage, your coins, unlocked tank paints, the selected paint, daily missions, the daily map's best score and your sound/vibration/language settings in a file on your device. Copies of this progress can be kept by Google, only in your own Google account:

  • Android backup – Android may include this file in your device backup to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app. You control this in your device settings (Settings → Google → Backup).
  • Google Play Games – if you use Play Games on your device, the app signs in to it automatically and stores your progress (best score, furthest stage, coins, unlocked paints) as a Play Games saved game, and submits your best score to the game's leaderboard, where other players can see it with your Play Games profile name. Google processes this under its privacy policy; you can manage or delete Play Games data in the Play Games app / your Google account.

Gameplay statistics (optional)

After your first game the app asks once whether you want to help improve the game by sending anonymous gameplay statistics. Nothing is sent unless you answer YES, and you can switch it off at any time with STATS ON / OFF in OPTIONS.

If you agree, the app sends:

  • game events: runs (score, stage, time, coins, what ended the run, tank paint), stages cleared, power-ups taken, the tutorial, missions, the daily map, CONTINUE / X2 offers, hangar and shop use (which item you tapped or bought – never payment details), app start and session length;
  • the game version, the app language, the operating-system version and the device model;
  • a random install ID created by the app – it is not linked to your Google account, your advertising ID or your device – and a random ID for each session.

The events go to our server, run for us by Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1; cloudflare.com/privacypolicy). It records the country derived from your IP address, but not the IP address itself. We use the statistics only to see how the game is played and what to improve – never for advertising – and we do not sell or share them. Events are deleted automatically after 13 months. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

Switching statistics off deletes the install ID and any events not yet sent from your device. Events already sent carry only the random install ID, so we cannot tell which of them are yours; they are deleted on schedule.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional in-app purchases (removing ads, a tank paint pack) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app only learns from Google Play which products your Google account owns, so they can be restored on reinstall or on a new device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob, and - only when you tap WATCH AD (for example to continue a run or double its coins) - a rewarded video ad. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • information about the app and the device (model, OS version, app version, crash and performance data),
  • interactions with ads.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and explains how it uses data from partner apps here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ads are requested, the app shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can accept, refuse or choose purposes, and change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button in the app’s menu.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Inchway ("the app") is a game published by **qkix** ("we"). This policy explains what
information is processed when you play it.

## Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or any account of our own. The only data that reach
our own server are the optional, anonymous gameplay statistics and race ghosts described below –
and only if you agree to send them or tap CHALLENGE.

The app saves your **scores, the top-10 records list, your coins, workshop tuning, unlocked car paints, the
selected paint and your sound/vibration settings** in a file on your device. Copies of this
progress can be kept by Google, only in your own Google account:

- **Android backup** – Android may include this file in your device backup to your Google
  account and restore it when you reinstall the app. You control this in your device settings
  (Settings → Google → Backup).
- **Google Play Games** – if you use Play Games on your device, the app signs in to it
  automatically and stores your progress (coins, unlocked paints, records) as a Play Games
  saved game, and submits your best score to the game's leaderboard, where other players can see
  it with your Play Games profile name. Google processes this under its privacy policy; you can
  manage or delete Play Games data in the Play Games app / your Google account.

## Gameplay statistics (optional)

After your first crash the app asks once whether you want to help improve the game by sending
anonymous gameplay statistics. **Nothing is sent unless you answer YES**, and you can switch it
off at any time with **STATS ON / OFF** in OPTIONS.

If you agree, the app sends:

- game events: runs (score, distance, time, coins, what you crashed into, the world, car paint
  and weather), tutorial hints shown, missions, garage, workshop and shop use (which item you tapped or
  bought – never payment details), app start and session length;
- the game version, the app language, the operating-system version and the device model;
- a random install ID created by the app – it is not linked to your Google account, your
  advertising ID or your device – and a random ID for each session.

The events go to our server, run for us by **Cloudflare** (Cloudflare Workers and D1;
<https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/>). It records the country derived from your IP address,
but not the IP address itself. We use the statistics only to see how the game is played and what
to improve – never for advertising – and we do not sell or share them. Events are **deleted
automatically after 13 months**. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

Switching statistics off deletes the install ID and any events not yet sent from your device.
Events already sent carry only the random install ID, so we cannot tell which of them are yours;
they are deleted on schedule.

## Race ghosts (CHALLENGE / RACE)

When you tap **CHALLENGE** after a run, the app shows that run's race code and sends a "ghost" of
the run to our server, stored under the code: your car's position across the road during the run,
the distance, the world, the car paint and the game version. It contains no ID of any kind – not
the statistics install ID, not your Google account – and nothing about your device. Anyone who
types the code in **RACE** gets the ghost and drives against it, so share the code only with
people you want to race. Nothing is sent before you tap CHALLENGE. Ghosts are stored on the same
Cloudflare service as the statistics and **deleted automatically after 90 days**. The legal basis is
your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): you asked the app to make the challenge.

## Purchases (Google Play)

Optional in-app purchases (removing ads, a car paint pack) are processed entirely by **Google
Play**. We never see your payment details; the app only learns from Google Play which products
your Google account owns, so they can be restored on reinstall or on a new device.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad provided by **Google AdMob**, and - only when you tap WATCH AD (for
example to continue a run or double its coins) - a rewarded video ad. To serve and measure ads, Google may
collect and process information from your device, such as:

- device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID,
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- information about the app and the device (model, OS version, app version, crash and
  performance data),
- interactions with ads.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy> and explains how it uses data from partner apps here:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

**Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** before any ads are requested, the app shows Google's
consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can accept, refuse or choose purposes. You can change
your choice at any time with the **PRIVACY** button in the app's menu.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device
settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from
them.

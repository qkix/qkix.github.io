This policy covers the **websites** of qkix: **qkix.eu** (this site) and **play.qkix.eu** (our games in the browser).
Each of our apps has its own policy, linked from its page under [qkix.eu/games](/games/).

## Who we are

qkix is a one-person studio from Poland. Contact for anything about your data: **contact@qkix.eu**.

## qkix.eu

- **Hosting:** the site is a static site served by **GitHub Pages** through **Cloudflare**. Like every web server they
  receive your IP address and the usual request data (page, browser, time) to deliver and protect the site; we do not
  get these logs and keep no visitor profiles.
- **No cookies of our own, no analytics.** The site stores one thing in your browser: the **language you picked**
  (local storage, it never leaves your device).
- **Videos:** trailers come from **YouTube** only after you click play (the page uses youtube-nocookie.com until then).
  Google then processes data under its own policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.
- **Ideas form** (on a game's roadmap page): the text you send, and a nickname and an e-mail address only if you type
  them (both optional – the e-mail only so we can answer you), go to our own server (a Cloudflare Worker) so we can read
  them. We delete them on request.

## play.qkix.eu – games in the browser

- **Your progress** (levels, scores, coins, settings) is saved **in your browser** (its local storage / IndexedDB). It
  never reaches us; clearing the site's data deletes it. There are no accounts, no logins and no purchases on the web.
- **Hosting:** the games are served by **Cloudflare Pages**; Cloudflare receives the usual request data to deliver them.
- **Anonymous game statistics:** a game may ask whether it may send anonymous gameplay events (e.g. "level 12 won in
  9 moves") to help us balance it. Nothing is sent unless you say yes; the events carry no name, account or device
  identifier.

## Advertising (Google AdSense) on play.qkix.eu

The browser games are free and show ads from **Google AdSense**: an ad between rounds now and then, and a video
**only when you choose** to watch one for a reward (a hint, a second chance, double coins).

- Visitors from the **European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland** see Google's consent message first and can
  **accept, refuse or choose** what ads may use. If you refuse, Google shows **non-personalised ads** only.
- **Games for children** (for example Hoplow) request **non-personalised ads** for everyone.
- To show, measure and protect ads, Google and its partners use **cookies and similar technologies** and process data
  such as your IP address, browser and device information and your interactions with ads. Learn how Google uses this
  data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>, and manage personalised ads in your Google account:
  <https://adssettings.google.com>. The list of Google's ad partners is linked from the consent message.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to processing or complain
to your data protection authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). For
anything we hold – practically only messages you sent us – write to **contact@qkix.eu**. For data processed by Google,
Cloudflare or GitHub, their own policies and controls apply.

We update this page when something changes and show the date at the top.
